# CHANGELOG — 2026-10-19

# Firm Wide Dashboard — Open Date when Matter Filed Date is blank

## What changed
- `backend/routes/firm_dashboard.py`
  - Totals, jurisdiction cards, and both trend charts now bucket on
    Matter Filed Date, and on Open Date when Filed Date is null or blank
    (`COALESCE(NULLIF(filed_date, ''), NULLIF(open_date, ''))`).
  - The "not included" count is only matters that have neither date.
- `frontend/src/pages/DashboardPage.jsx`
  - Chart axis label is "Filed Date (Open Date if blank)".
  - The note under Firm Wide Matter Totals now describes matters missing
    both dates.

## Why
About 1,207 cached matters had no Matter Filed Date and were left out of
every date-based total. Open Date is already stored on those rows, so no
new Clio refresh is required for them to be counted.

## Verification
- SQLite check: a matter with a filed date, a matter with only an open date,
  and a matter with a blank filed date plus an open date all land in the
  selected window and in the correct month. A matter with neither date is
  the only one excluded.
